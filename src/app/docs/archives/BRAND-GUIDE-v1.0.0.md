# BRAND GUIDE

**Project:** Heroes or Henchmen  
**Universe:** Heroes or Henchmen  
**Organization:** The Order  
**Document:** Brand Guide  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** FOUNDATION FROZEN

---

# 1. PURPOSE

This document defines the official identity of **The Order**.

It establishes the principles that govern how The Order is perceived across every interface, artifact, document, archive, symbol and communication within the Heroes or Henchmen universe.

This document defines:

- Brand Philosophy
- Brand Personality
- Visual Principles
- Communication Principles
- Brand Voice
- Symbolic Hierarchy
- Experience Principles
- Rules of Consistency

This document does not define implementation details.

It does not define:

- Angular architecture
- Component structure
- SVG implementation
- Layout geometry
- Rendering pipelines
- File structures
- Software services

Those responsibilities belong to their respective technical and design specifications.

---

# 2. BRAND FOUNDATION

The Order is the institutional presence at the center of the Heroes or Henchmen universe.

Its purpose is not to create destinies.

Its purpose is not to judge individuals.

Its purpose is not to determine what is right or wrong.

The Order observes.

The Order preserves.

The Order records.

The Order protects.

The Order guards what must not be forgotten.

At the center of its existence stands the **Prisma da Escolha**.

The Prisma does not create an essence.

It does not impose a path.

It does not judge.

It reveals.

The Order exists to preserve the knowledge surrounding that revelation.

---

# 3. BRAND PHILOSOPHY

The identity of The Order is built upon five foundational principles.

## 3.1 AUTHORITY

The Order speaks with certainty.

Never aggressively.

Never emotionally.

Never theatrically.

Its authority comes from knowledge, preservation and continuity.

The Order does not need to prove its existence.

It simply exists.

---

## 3.2 PRECISION

Everything recorded by The Order exists for a reason.

Nothing is included without purpose.

Nothing is decorative without meaning.

Information must be clear.

Symbols must be intentional.

Classification must be precise.

The Order does not speculate when observation is possible.

---

## 3.3 MYSTERY

The Order does not reveal everything at once.

Knowledge is discovered gradually.

Questions may remain unanswered.

Some records may be incomplete.

Some origins may remain unknown.

Mystery is not confusion.

Mystery is the presence of knowledge that has not yet been revealed.

The Order never manufactures mystery through ambiguity without purpose.

It preserves mystery by acknowledging what is unknown.

---

## 3.4 PERMANENCE

The Order existed before the observer.

The Order will continue after the observer is gone.

Its identity must therefore feel timeless.

It must not depend on contemporary trends.

It must not feel bound to a specific technological era.

It should feel as though its records could have existed centuries ago and could still exist centuries from now.

---

## 3.5 NEUTRALITY

The Order does not belong to any single Essence.

The five Personalities are not created by The Order.

They are recognized by it.

The Order does not declare one Essence superior to another.

It does not determine which path is good or evil.

It observes the resonance revealed by the Prisma.

Therefore, the institutional identity of The Order must remain visually neutral.

The colors associated with individual Personalities belong to the Essences.

They do not belong to The Order.

---

# 4. BRAND PERSONALITY

The Order is:

- Intelligent
- Ancient
- Methodical
- Silent
- Precise
- Neutral
- Protective
- Observational
- Institutional
- Timeless

The Order is never:

- Loud
- Chaotic
- Playful
- Corporate
- Promotional
- Aggressive
- Excessively futuristic
- Excessively fantastical
- Decorative for decoration's sake

---

# 5. VISUAL KEYWORDS

The visual identity of The Order should evoke:

- Institution
- Archive
- Preservation
- Geometry
- Precision
- Balance
- Knowledge
- Observation
- Memory
- Classification
- Discovery
- Permanence
- Containment
- Unknown Origins

These concepts form the visual vocabulary of the brand.

---

# 6. DESIGN LANGUAGE

Every visual manifestation of The Order should communicate the following sequence:

```text
ORDER
   ↓
KNOWLEDGE
   ↓
DOCUMENTATION
   ↓
OBSERVATION
   ↓
DISCOVERY
   ↓
PRESERVATION
```

The visual language must never reverse this hierarchy.

The Order does not exist to entertain.

It exists to reveal a world that already exists.

---

# 7. VISUAL PRINCIPLES

## 7.1 GEOMETRY

Geometry represents order and precision.

Visual structures should favor:

- Balance
- Symmetry
- Proportion
- Repetition
- Alignment
- Controlled complexity

Geometry must feel intentional.

---

## 7.2 HIERARCHY

Every element possesses a degree of importance.

The viewer must understand what deserves attention first.

Institutional identity must never be visually subordinate to decorative elements.

Visual hierarchy should communicate:

```text
THE ORDER
    ↓
THE CODEX
    ↓
THE ARTIFACT
    ↓
THE GLYPH
    ↓
DECORATIVE ELEMENTS
```

The institution always comes first.

---

## 7.3 RESTRAINT

The Order does not display power unnecessarily.

Effects, animation, illumination and visual complexity must be controlled.

The absence of an effect may communicate more authority than the effect itself.

Visual restraint is a sign of confidence.

---

## 7.4 CONSISTENCY

Every interface must feel as though it belongs to the same institution.

A viewer should be able to recognize the presence of The Order before reading any text.

Consistency is more important than novelty.

New visual ideas must strengthen the existing language rather than compete with it.

---

# 8. COLOR PHILOSOPHY

Color is semantic.

Never merely decorative.

The colors of The Order communicate institutional states, classifications and hierarchy.

Individual Essence colors belong to the Personalities.

The official Personalities are:

| Essence | Color |
|---|---|
| Hero | Blue |
| Protector | Green |
| Vigilante | Purple |
| Mercenary | Gold |
| Henchmen | Red |

These colors represent the Essences.

They do not define the institutional identity of The Order.

The Order itself must remain visually neutral.

Its institutional palette may be represented through:

- Neutral metallic tones
- Obsidian
- White
- Silver
- Aged gold
- Controlled monochrome values

Individual Essence colors may appear when the Prisma enters resonance or when an Essence is being represented.

---

# 9. TYPOGRAPHY PHILOSOPHY

Typography communicates authority and permanence.

The typographic language should prioritize:

- Legibility
- Stability
- Precision
- Institutional character
- Hierarchical clarity

Primary typography should feel established and authoritative.

Secondary typography may communicate:

- Metadata
- Registry information
- Codes
- Coordinates
- Technical records
- System information

Monospaced typography may be used for technical or archival metadata.

Decorative typography must never compromise readability.

Typography should support the institution.

It must never become the institution.

---

# 10. SYMBOLIC LANGUAGE

Symbols are fundamental to The Order.

Every symbol must possess meaning.

The visual language may include:

- Seals
- Sigils
- Glyphs
- Runes
- Geometric marks
- Registration symbols
- Classification marks
- Security indicators

These symbols must not be treated as decoration.

They represent concepts, identities, classifications or institutional functions.

---

# 11. GLYPHS

Glyphs are conceptual symbols.

They are not logos.

They are not illustrations.

They are not decorative icons.

A Glyph represents an idea, property or conceptual identity.

Possible concepts include:

- Origin
- Balance
- Containment
- Observation
- Connection
- Memory
- Energy
- Transformation

Glyphs form part of the symbolic vocabulary of The Order.

Individual Glyphs may evolve or expand as the universe grows, provided that they remain consistent with the established visual language.

---

# 12. SIGILS

Sigils represent the five Personalities revealed through the Prisma.

The Order did not create the Sigils.

The Order recognizes and preserves them.

Each Personality possesses its own:

- Sigil
- Rune
- Color
- Glow
- Geometry
- Identity

The Sigils belong to the Essences.

They do not belong to The Order.

The institutional identity of The Order must therefore remain neutral in relation to them.

---

# 13. THE PRISMA

The Prisma da Escolha is the central artifact of the universe.

Its function is revelation.

It does not create.

It does not judge.

It does not impose.

It reveals.

The Prisma is visually neutral while dormant.

When resonance occurs, its visual manifestation may reflect the Essence being revealed.

The visual language of the Prisma must communicate:

- Mystery
- Precision
- Observation
- Revelation
- Energy
- Neutrality

The Prisma must never appear as a simple decorative object.

Its presence carries narrative significance.

---

# 14. THE CODEX

The Codex Vaticinarum is the institutional archive of The Order.

It is not merely a book.

It is not a conventional database.

It is a record of knowledge preserved by the institution.

The Codex should feel:

- Ancient
- Official
- Authenticated
- Incomplete in places
- Precise
- Mysterious
- Permanent

The Codex documents what The Order knows.

It may also reveal what The Order does not know.

Unknown information is part of the archive.

An incomplete record is preferable to an invented truth.

---

# 15. ARTIFACT IDENTITY

Artifacts possess their own identities.

The Order documents them.

It does not imitate them.

An artifact may possess its own:

- Geometry
- Symbol
- Energy
- Color
- History
- Classification

The institutional identity of The Order remains constant regardless of the artifact being documented.

---

# 16. MOTION LANGUAGE

Motion must communicate purpose.

Approved meanings include:

- Activation
- Scanning
- Discovery
- Observation
- Confirmation
- Resonance
- Containment
- Revelation

Motion must remain controlled.

Animations must never feel like entertainment.

Motion must never distract from information.

The slower the revelation, the greater the perceived importance.

---

# 17. SOUND PHILOSOPHY

If sound is introduced, it must reinforce the identity of The Order.

Sound may evoke:

- Instrumentation
- Ancient mechanisms
- Mechanical systems
- Signal transmission
- Resonance
- Energy
- Containment

Sound must never feel:

- Arcade-like
- Comedic
- Promotional
- Excessively fantastical
- Generic science fiction

Silence is also part of the identity.

Not every interaction requires sound.

---

# 18. IMAGERY

Imagery should appear:

- Archived
- Catalogued
- Observed
- Recovered
- Documented
- Classified

Images should feel like evidence or records rather than promotional material.

The Order does not advertise.

It documents.

---

# 19. BRAND VOICE

The voice of The Order is:

- Objective
- Formal
- Precise
- Concise
- Observational
- Institutional
- Restrained

The Order does not exaggerate.

The Order does not persuade.

The Order does not seek approval.

The Order records.

---

# 20. LANGUAGE PRINCIPLES

The Order speaks from a position of observation.

Prefer:

> "The record indicates..."

> "The artifact was recovered..."

> "The available evidence suggests..."

> "The origin remains unknown."

> "Classification confirmed."

Avoid:

> "We think..."

> "We believe..."

> "This is amazing..."

> "You are..."

The language must preserve institutional distance.

---

# 21. THE GUARDIAN

The Guardian occupies a unique position within the universe.

The Guardian is associated with The Order and the protection of the Prisma.

However, the identity, origin and nature of the Guardian remain undisclosed.

The brand must never force an explanation where the universe has intentionally preserved uncertainty.

The Guardian should therefore be represented through:

- Presence
- Authority
- Records
- Signatures
- Protocols
- Actions

Not through unnecessary exposition.

Mystery is preserved through absence of confirmation.

---

# 22. USER EXPERIENCE

The observer should feel:

- Curious
- Guided
- Safe
- Respected
- Intrigued

The observer should never feel:

- Lost
- Manipulated
- Overwhelmed
- Forced into a conclusion

The experience should invite discovery.

The Order does not tell the observer what to think.

It provides the evidence.

---

# 23. REVELATION PRINCIPLE

Information should be revealed progressively.

The experience should move through:

```text
OBSERVATION
    ↓
CURIOSITY
    ↓
DISCOVERY
    ↓
RESONANCE
    ↓
REVELATION
    ↓
UNDERSTANDING
```

Not every mystery must be resolved immediately.

Some questions should remain open.

The unknown is part of the universe.

---

# 24. BRAND CONSISTENCY

Every new interface, artifact, document, symbol or interaction must be recognizable as belonging to The Order.

This recognition should occur before the observer reads the text.

If an element could exist in any unrelated project without modification, it should be reconsidered.

The identity of The Order must remain unmistakable.

---

# 25. GOLDEN RULE

Every visual, narrative or experiential decision must answer one question:

> **"Does this strengthen the identity of The Order?"**

If the answer is no,

the decision must be reconsidered.

If the answer is yes,

it may be evaluated for inclusion without violating the established canon.

---

# 26. CANONICAL STATEMENT

The Order does not create what it guards.

It does not judge what it observes.

It does not impose what it reveals.

It preserves.

It records.

It protects.

And when the Prisma awakens,

The Order does not choose the path.

It merely bears witness to what was already there.
